Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a lost item?
Login to your account, click on "Report Lost Item", fill the item details and submit.
How do I claim a found item?
Go to the Found Items page, select the item and submit a claim request.
Who can use this system?
Only registered students and staff of the institution can use the system.
How will I know if my item is found?
You will receive a notification or email once the item is reported.
