Lost And Found Management System For Olabisi Onabanjo University Community
Helping you reunite with your lost items

🎁 Key Benefits of This System

Improved Item Recovery Rate The system increases the chances of recovering lost items by connecting users who lost items with those who found them through a centralized platform
Time Efficiency Instead of physically searching or asking around campus, users can quickly report and search for items online, saving time and effort
Easy Accessibility Users can access the system anytime and anywhere, making it convenient for students and staff within the university environment
User-Friendly Interaction The platform is designed to be simple and easy to use, allowing even non-technical users to use

HOW IT WORKS

01

LOGIN

Users begin by creating an account or logging into the system.
This ensures that all activities are tracked and linked to a verified user.

02

CREATE A LISTING

After logging in, the user creates a listing depending on their situation if they misplaced or discovered an item

03

SEARCH

Users can explore available listings by Searching with keywords & Filtering by location or item type

04

MATCH & CLAIM

When a user finds a matching item they initiate a claim request providing proof of ownership (details only the real owner would know)

05

ADMIN VERIFICATION

An administrator reviews the claim to:Confirm ownership authenticity, Prevent false claims & Ensure system integrity

06

ITEM RECOVERY

Once approved, The rightful owner is notified, A pickup or meeting is arranged
